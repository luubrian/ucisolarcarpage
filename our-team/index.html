The people behind the car

Our Team

Our Subteams

Mechanical

The Chassis & Aerobody subteam focuses on designing a lightweight, durable chassis encapsulating all subsystems and an aeroshell for optimized aerodynamics. The Suspension subteam is dedicated to designing the front and rear suspension and steering system. The Human Interface subsystem oversees the mechanical systems the driver interacts with, including the seat, driver harness, pedal box, braking systems, and dashboard.

Our Subteams

Electrical

The High Voltage subteam focuses on essential components such as the powertrain, solar array, and battery pack, integral to powering our vehicle. Meanwhile, the Low Voltage team manages embedded systems, programming the user's dashboard, controls, wireless communication, and handling safety shutdown operations.

Our Subteams

Operations

The Operations team takes charge of efficiently managing financial resources and implementing marketing strategies for the team. Their responsibilities include fostering community outreach, developing corporate connections, exploring and planning funding opportunities, and maintaining the team's website and social media presence.
